How it works

  1. Click around the area where faces should be recognised. Everything outside the shape is ignored.
  2. Points are saved at the camera's full resolution, with the picture size.
  3. Without a region, the whole picture is used.
  4. Restart the camera services after saving: producer-consumer and the two controllers, on the edge node if one captures.
