    What recognition sees

    Guidance

    1. Pencil: click to add corner points, or hold and drag to trace the area freehand; hold Shift while dragging for a straight line. Long strokes are smoothed to at most 100 points.
    2. Line: drag from a point to draw a straight line to where you let go, one side at a time. The outline stays open while you draw; click the first point, or switch to Edit, to close it.
    3. Edit: drag a corner point, drag inside the shape to move it, or drag a square handle to scale it. + and − grow or shrink it by 10%.
    4. Everything outside the shape is ignored; without a region the whole picture is used. Points are stored at full resolution with the picture size.
    5. Saving does not apply it yet: on an edge node, copy the camera configuration there first, then restart producer-consumer and the two controllers.
